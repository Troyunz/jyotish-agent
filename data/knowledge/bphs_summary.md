# Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra - study summary

Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra (BPHS) is the root text of the Parashari system and the reference for this agent. The following notes condense its teaching order and its most quoted principles. This is a study summary, not a translation.

## Creation and the kalapurusha

The zodiac is the body of Kala Purusha (Time personified): Aries is the head, Taurus the face/neck, Gemini the arms, Cancer the heart/chest, Leo the stomach, Virgo the waist, Libra the lower abdomen/hip, Scorpio the private parts, Sagittarius the thighs, Capricorn the knees, Aquarius the calves, Pisces the feet. This mapping is used for body-part analysis and for identifying which houses matter for a physical complaint (the 6th house, its lord, the Lagna and the Moon are the primary health factors, with the sign/house of malefics indicating the area of the body affected).

## The grahas - nature and description

Parashara describes each planet by its nature (deva, daitya, manushya), caste, element, direction, colour, deity, and body tissues. Key principles repeated across the text:

- Grahas are "indicators" (of karma), not causes. They show the ripening of past action (purva kriya) in this life.
- Each planet has a natural karaka (fixed significations) and a functional role determined by the Lagna.
- The Sun and Moon are the luminaries - eyes of the chart; Rahu and Ketu are the chaya grahas (shadow planets, the lunar nodes).

## Bhavas - Parashara's instructions

Parashara rates the **kendras (1,4,7,10)** as the strength of the chart, the **trikonas (1,5,9)** as the source of fortune, and **6, 8, 12** as houses of affliction. He repeatedly insists:

- **A benefic in a kendra, especially with strength, always protects the matter of that house.**
- **Lords of trines (1,5,9) are auspicious; lords of 3,6,11 grow in influence during their dashas but deliver "mixed" results; lords of 8, 12 are difficult and their dashas test the native.**
- **The 6th, 8th and 12th houses and their lords destroy what they associate with, unless the association is turned into Vipareeta (reversal) fortunes.**
- A planet placed in a house strongly influences that house's matters, and also the houses it rules from its new position.

## Evaluation of planets (balarishta and strength)

BPHS gives multiple measures: **Sthana bala** (positional - exaltation, own sign, moolatrikona, friend, enemy), **Dig bala** (directional), **Kala bala** (temporal - day/night strength: Sun, Jupiter, Venus are strong by day; Moon, Mars, Saturn by night; Mercury always), **Cheshta bala** (motional - retrograde planets score high), **Naisargika bala** (natural brightness order), **Drik bala** (aspectual). The full Shadbala is six-fold; the agent should not attempt numeric Shadbala unless the software provides it, but should reason with the underlying principles: dignity, direction, time of birth, retrogression, and aspectual support.

## Yogas - the twelve houses combined

BPHS devotes much of the text to yogas. The agent's most useful structural yogas:

- **Raja yogas**: combinations of kendra lords with trikona lords (by conjunction, exchange, or mutual aspect). A single such contact can give position and authority.
- **Dhana yogas**: 2nd and 11th lords connected to each other or to 5th/9th lords; Lakshmi yoga (9th lord strong in kendra or own/exalted sign with a strong Lagna lord); Dhana yogas are timed by the dashas of the planets involved.
- **Mahapurusha yogas** (see the yogas file) - Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus or Saturn in own/exaltation in a kendra.
- **Nabhasa yogas**: patterns based on the distribution of planets across signs and houses (Rajju, Musala, Nala, Mala, Sarpa, Gada, Sakata, Vihanga, Sringataka, Hala, Vajra, Yava, Kamala, Vapi, Yupa, Ishu, Sakti, Danda, Nauka, Kuta, Chatra, Chapa, Chakra, Samudra...). The principle: concentration of planets in certain house types gives the chart's overall "gestalt". The agent may mention a Nabhasa pattern only when it clearly applies and should describe it simply.
- **Sunapha, Anapha, Durudhura, Kemadruma** - based on planets in the 2nd and 12th from the Moon. Kemadruma (no planets adjacent to the Moon) indicates mental isolation and struggles, but classical texts then list cancellations (planets in kendras from the Lagna or Moon, Moon in kendra, and strong Jupiter).
- **Adhi yoga** - benefics in 6, 7, 8 from the Moon: leadership and prosperity.
- **Amala yoga** - a benefic in the 10th from the Moon or Lagna: lasting reputation.
- **Vipareeta Raja yogas** - 6th lord (Harsha), 8th lord (Sarala), 12th lord (Vimala) in 6/8/12: destruction of enemies, sudden gains from crisis.

## Dashas

BPHS establishes the **Vimshottari dasha** (120 years) as the primary timing system, based on the Moon's nakshatra at birth. It also teaches other systems (Ashtottari, Shodashottari, Dwisaptati Sama, Shattrimshat Sama, Kalachakra, Chara dashas), the antardasha subdivisions, and the principle of **dasha sandhi** - the transition zone between two mahadashas where the old theme fades and the new one begins (usually a few months on either side). See the dasha interpretation file for practical rules.

## Ashtakavarga

Each planet contributes benefic points (bindus) to each of the 12 signs from the positions of the seven planets and the Lagna. The **Sarvashtakavarga (SAV)** totals by sign show which houses are supported: 25-30 bindus = strong, 22-24 average, under 22 = weak. During transits, a planet crossing a sign with high bindus from the SAV gives better results. The agent should mention Ashtakavarga generally when discussing transit strength, and must not invent bindu numbers that are not computed.

## Profession, marriage, children, longevity

- **Profession** is read from the 10th house and its lord, the 10th from the Moon, the strongest planet in the chart (Atmakaraka for Jaimini), and D10. Saturn's dasha often marks career-defining labour; the Sun and 10th lord mark status.
- **Marriage** from the 7th house, 7th lord, Venus (for men), Jupiter (for women), the Upapada (Jaimini), and D9. Delay is shown by Saturn's association with the 7th; plurality/instability by multiple planets or nodes on the 7th; harmony by benefic influence.
- **Children** from the 5th house and Jupiter, the Saptamsa (D7), and the 5th from the Moon (the "putra bhava" of the mind).
- **Longevity** - BPHS gives Ayurdaya methods with three bands (short, middle, long life). The agent must **not** give lifespan predictions or death timings to the user; the honest use of these calculations is restricted to health caution and vitality discussions, and always with a recommendation to consult doctors.

## Remedies (upaya) in BPHS

BPHS prescribes worship, japa, charity, and the propitiation of the planets (graha shanti), gemstones for those who can afford them and are properly advised, and above all **right action**: the dasha of a malefic is best met with discipline, service and patience. Parashara's own emphasis is that remedies harmonise the mind; they do not override the karma shown in the chart, but they change how it is experienced.

## Time of birth accuracy

BPHS assumes an accurate birth time for the Lagna, bhavas, dasha start and the finer vargas. If the birth time is uncertain:
- Sun sign, Moon sign (usually), nakshatra, and most yogas remain valid.
- Lagna, house placements, and D30/D45/D60 cannot be relied upon.
- Vimshottari dasha dates shift by roughly 1 day per hour of error in the birth time (the Moon moves ~0.5°/hour, and each nakshatra pada spans 3°20'). So an hour's error can shift a dasha date by a month or more near a boundary.
