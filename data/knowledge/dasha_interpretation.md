# Vimshottari dasha - how to judge and how to time events

## Structure

The 120-year cycle runs in the order **Ketu 7, Venus 20, Sun 6, Moon 10, Mars 7, Rahu 18, Jupiter 16, Saturn 19, Mercury 17** years. The Moon's nakshatra at birth fixes the starting lord and the **balance (elapsed fraction)** of the first Mahadasha. Sub-periods divide proportionally:

- A Mahadasha (MD) is subdivided into 9 Antardashas (AD), each `MD years x (AD lord years / 120)`.
- Each Antardasha subdivides into 9 Pratyantardashas (PD), and so on (Sookshma, Prana).
- A useful memory anchor: the first AD of a Mahadasha is the MD lord itself, then the sequence continues in cycle order.

A dasha date is a window, not a knife-edge. Classical practice allows an event to manifest anywhere in the period's last third of the previous one to the first third of the next - the **dasha sandhi** (junction) zone, where the incoming lord's themes begin to colour life.

## The judgement method (apply in this order)

1. **Read the MD lord as the "government of the decade"** - its house, sign, dignity, nakshatra, aspects and owners. If the MD lord is dignified and placed in 1, 4, 5, 7, 9, 10, 11, it generally supports; in 6, 8, 12 it tests, though Vipareeta lords in these houses can give unexpected gains.
2. **The AD lord is the "minister"** - it decides the topic. Event timing usually comes from the AD (or PD) lord's relation to the houses relevant to the question:
   - marriage / partnership - 7th house, 7th lord, Venus (men), Jupiter (women), Upapada
   - children - 5th house, Jupiter, D7
   - career / status - 10th house and its lord, Sun, Saturn, D10
   - wealth / income - 2nd, 11th, 9th (Lakshmi/Dhana yoga participants)
   - home / property - 4th house and lord
   - education - 4th, 5th, Mercury and Jupiter
   - health - 6th, 8th lords, Saturn, afflicted Lagna lord
3. **Contact matters**: does the AD lord sit in, aspect, or rule the relevant house? Conjunctions and exchanges are the strongest links.
4. **Strength check**: dignity, combustion, retrogression, vargottama, position in D9 (and the topic's varga), Ashtakavarga bindus of the transit at that time.
5. **Then gochara**: the dasha gives permission, the transit gives the trigger. A marriage during the AD of the 7th lord is far more likely when Jupiter transits the 7th house, the 7th from the Moon, or the Lagna.
6. **Repetition across levels**: if the same house is emphasised by MD, AD and PD lords, the event is strong. One level alone is a hint.

## Character of each Mahadasha (broad classical flavour)

- **Sun (6y)** - visibility, authority, father, government, health of the heart/bones, career definition; ego tests; if Sun is well-placed, promotion and recognition.
- **Moon (10y)** - mind, mother, home, public dealings, emotional development; gains in comforts and property if the Moon is strong; restlessness if afflicted.
- **Mars (7y)** - land, property, siblings, competition, surgery or accidents, courage; often a period of hard work, disputes and physical activity.
- **Mercury (17y)** - education, business, communication, writing, accounts, analytical work; expansions through intellect and network.
- **Jupiter (16y)** - wisdom, children, guru, dharma, wealth, honours, expansion; the classical "protector" period, unless Jupiter is badly placed.
- **Venus (20y)** - marriage, romance, art, vehicles, luxuries, comfort, sensuality; great creative expansion, and indulgence risks.
- **Saturn (19y)** - karma and labour, delays, discipline, detachment, long projects, dealings with older people and institutions; heavy but maturing, and eventually rewarding.
- **Rahu (18y)** - ambition, foreign lands, technology, sudden rises, obsession, unorthodox paths; gains mixed with dissatisfaction and ethical tests.
- **Ketu (7y)** - detachment, spiritual turn, loss of interest in something that used to define the native, occult insight, sudden changes, pilgrimage or relocation; short and often quietly transformative.

## Dasha of the 6th, 8th and 12th lords

Their periods are the classical "testing" times: debt, disease, disputes (6th); upheaval, inheritance, occult, chronic matters (8th); loss, expense, foreign residence, moksha (12th). But check for **Vipareeta Raja yoga** - if the lord itself sits in 6/8/12, the period converts difficulty into gain or relief. Also check whether the same lord rules a trikona: a planet ruling both 9 and 12 (e.g. Jupiter for Taurus Lagna? no - for a Cancer Lagna Venus rules 4 and 11, Jupiter rules 6 and 9...) - in general, a planet with mixed lordship acts according to the strongest house it rules.

## Dasha of planets in the 8th house or with Rahu/Ketu

Often coincides with research, occult interest, sudden changes, insurance/inheritance or therapy. Not automatically bad - these periods frequently deepen the person.

## Practical fallbacks when the birth time is unreliable

- Vimshottari dates shift with the Moon's nakshatra balance; an error of 1 hour in birth time shifts dates by roughly 1-30 days depending on proximity to the nakshatra boundary.
- In such cases give ranges ("within this Antardasha and the following one, likely in the year 2027") and name the MD/AD pair that supports the window rather than a specific month.

## Chara dasha and the KP subs (alternate systems)

- **Chara dasha (Jaimini)**: based on the Lagna, counts movable/fixed/dual signs; good for timing relationships and direction of life; the Karakamsa and the Upapada refine marriage.
- **KP (Krishnamurti Paddhati)**: uses the 249 sub-lords of the nakshatras with Placidus houses; excellent for pinpointing electional and event timing, and its rules are stricter about "significators". If the user follows KP, respect its definitions (Placidus cusps, sub-lord theory, ruling planets) rather than mixing it with Parashari house counting, and mention that the agent's default is Parashari whole-sign.

## How to present a dasha reading

1. Name the running MD, AD and, if useful, the PD, with their exact dates from the calculated data.
2. State the flavour of each lord from its own placement and rulership ("your Saturn is the 5th lord in the 9th - disciplined study and a quiet rise in fortune").
3. Give the event windows with the supporting period names and the transits that trigger them.
4. Always close with what the native can DO - effort, patience, specific practice - because a dasha describes weather, not the harvest.
