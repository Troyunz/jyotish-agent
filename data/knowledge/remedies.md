# Traditional remedies (upaya) - what Jyotisha prescribes

Remedies are described here as the tradition describes them. They are complementary practices that steady the mind and align conduct; they are not a substitute for medical treatment, financial advice, or professional help, and the agent must always say so when recommending any.

## The classical hierarchy

1. **Right conduct first (karma upaya)** - the strongest remedy in the tradition is living according to the dharma of the chart: honesty in the afflicted house's domain, discipline during a hard dasha, service where the 6th/8th/12th houses are weak.
2. **Japa (mantra repetition)** - steady recitation of the graha mantra or the ishta devata mantra.
3. **Daan (charity)** - giving the item associated with the planet that needs propitiation, on that planet's day.
4. **Vrata (observance)** - fasting (light, health-permitting) on the planet's weekday, and temple observance.
5. **Puja / homa** - Navagraha puja, planet-specific homas, Hanuman Chalisa for Mars and Saturn.
6. **Ratna (gemstones)** - supportive stones for a benefic planet; **worn only after professional consultation**, and the classical texts warn especially about blue sapphire for Saturn, which must be trialled.
7. **Rudraksha** - sanctified beads worn for specific planets.
8. **Ishta devata** - the deity indicated by the 12th house from the Karakamsa (Jaimini) or by the strongest benefic; worship of that deity is considered the deepest remedy for moksha and peace.

## Remedies matched to specific difficulties

- **Sade Sati / heavy Saturn dasha** - Hanuman worship (Chalisa or Sundarakanda on Saturdays), Shani Stotra, service to the elderly and to labourers, black sesame oil lamp under a peepal tree on Saturday, honesty in the domain of the house Saturn is testing, patience as the primary upaya.
- **Mangal dosha / hard Mars periods** - Hanuman Chalisa on Tuesdays, red coral only if Mars is a functional benefic, physical discipline (exercise, martial arts), feeding monkeys/birds, controlling speech and anger.
- **Rahu / Ketu periods** - Durga and Ganesha worship, Bhairav upasana, feeding dogs (Ketu), donating blankets, avoiding shortcuts and intoxication, keeping speech and dealings clean.
- **Weak Moon / anxiety** - Monday observance, Shiva worship with milk and water, Om Namah Shivaya, meditation, time near water, care of the mother and of one's own mind through rest.
- **Guru's grace when Jupiter is weak** - Thursday fasting (light), turmeric and chana dal daan, serving teachers and students, sponsoring education, reading of scripture or Bhagavad Gita.
- **Venus afflictions (marital friction)** - Friday Lakshmi worship, Shri Suktam, respect and care toward the partner as the true remedy, artistic practice, cleanliness and beauty in the home.
- **Health indications (6th/8th houses)** - the classical remedy is discipline in food, sleep and routine plus the medical care of a doctor; mantras for the afflicted planet accompany treatment, never replace it.
- **Education/study blocks (Mercury/Jupiter)** - Saraswati or Ganesha worship, Vishnu Sahasranama, charity of books, teaching others what one has learned.
- **Obstacles in career (10th house afflictions)** - Sun worship (water offering at sunrise - arghya), service to the workplace and colleagues, honesty in accounts and contracts, work-related skill development (karma upaya).
- **Marriage delay** - worship of the 7th house deity and of Gauri/Parvati, Jupiter and Venus remedies, respect toward marriage-minded family elders, and practical openness (the tradition holds that remedies remove obstacles, not that they force events).

## Yogic kriyas prescribed as remedies (not Jyotisha techniques)

Some practices prescribed by astrologers come from **Yoga**, not from Jyotisha itself. They are
legitimate *upayas* while remaining outside the astrological canon - say so when prescribing them, so
the person knows what they are receiving and from which tradition.

- **Tratak (fixed gazing)** - one of the six shatkarmas of Hatha Yoga and a concentration practice in
  Raja Yoga; classically a steady flame or a distant point is gazed at without blinking. Astrologers
  commonly prescribe it for a weak or afflicted **Moon** (restlessness, anxiety, sleeplessness),
  for **Rahu/Ketu** afflictions (scattered attention, obsession), and for the 6th/8th/12th-house
  mental strain. Standard beginner form: 1-2 minutes, eyes then closed and the after-image held in
  the mind's eye, building slowly; done facing east, ideally at dawn. It should not be practised with
  eye disease, glaucoma, epilepsy, migraine in an active phase, or while pregnant without medical
  advice, and never to the point of eye strain. Its purpose here is steadying the mind, not
  occult power.
- **Pranayama and the shatkarmas** (neti, nauli, kapalabhati and the rest) - likewise Yoga, and
  likewise prescribed remedially for planetary afflictions of the mind and body. The same caution
  applies: these are practices with real physiological effects and belong with a teacher, not a
  chart reading.
- **Asana, yama and niyama** - the classical texts treat right living itself as the deepest remedy;
  the planetary "remedy" is often simply restoring routine and conduct.

## Cautions the agent must always add

- Gemstones and yantras can be expensive; a poor fitting can, by tradition, aggravate the matter. Recommend them only for a benefic planet, after consulting a qualified astrologer or jeweller-priest, and with the option of a trial period.
- No remedy can promise a specific outcome, cure an illness, guarantee wealth, or override free will and professional advice.
- Fear-based remedies ("do this or disaster will follow") are contrary to the spirit of the shastras; the tone must always be that of steadying the mind and improving conduct.
