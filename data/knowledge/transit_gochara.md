# Gochara - planetary transits in Jyotisha

## The rule of reference

Transits are counted **from the natal Moon (janma rashi)** for general life effects, and from the **Lagna** for matters of the body, health and personality. North Indian practice almost always uses the Moon sign; always state which reference you are using. The gochara chart is calculated in the sidereal zodiac with the same ayanamsa as the natal chart. Results consider:

1. the house the transiting planet occupies from the natal Moon (and Lagna),
2. the nature of the transiting planet (natural benefic or malefic),
3. the strength of the natal Moon and the natal house being crossed,
4. **Vedha** (obstruction) points - certain houses "obstruct" the good results of other houses; classical texts (e.g. Phaladeepika's gochara chapter) give tables of these; mention vedha only where the classical table is known,
5. **Ashtakavarga bindus** of the sign being crossed - more bindus, less friction,
6. the dasha running at the same time - the dasha gives permission, transits trigger.

## Saturn (the most watched transit)

Saturn stays about 2.5 years in a sign; its full cycle is ~29.5 years.

- **From the natal Moon:**
  - 1st - pressure on the mind and body, responsibility, austerity
  - 2nd - family and finances get tight; measured speech needed
  - 3rd - one of Saturn's best positions: effort pays, courage, gains from hard work
  - 4th - domestic unrest or relocation; mother's health; emotional heaviness
  - 5th - delays or responsibilities concerning children, studies, creativity
  - 6th - excellent: enemies and debts are overcome, health improves through discipline
  - 7th - pressure on partnership; patience in marriage; good for business endurance
  - 8th - the most testing: health vigilance, obstacles, but deep lessons; "Ashtama Shani"
  - 9th - upheaval of fortune and beliefs; father's health; pilgrimage suggested
  - 10th - heavy work, career pressure, promotion through labour ("Kantaka Shani")
  - 11th - gains, income, fulfilment of ambitions; one of the best
  - 12th - expenses, foreign matters, spiritual turn, sleep issues
- **Sade Sati** - Saturn transiting the 12th, 1st and 2nd from the natal Moon: a seven-and-a-half-year maturation. First phase (12th) - expenses and quiet pressure; peak (over the Moon) - emotional intensity, restructuring of identity; final phase (2nd) - family, finances and speech get restructured. Classical relief: Saturn's strong natal position, an exalted or own-sign Moon, benefic aspects on the Moon, and the Sade Sati often ends with markedly more maturity and stability than before it began. Present it as a rigorous teacher, never as a curse.
- **Saturn return (age ~29, ~58, ~87)** - a defining restructuring of career and identity, whether or not Sade Sati runs.
- Saturn is "strong in transit" in the 3rd, 6th and 11th from the Moon, and testing in the 1st, 2nd, 4th, 8th, 12th.

## Jupiter (the great benefic transit)

Jupiter spends about 1 year in a sign; its cycle is ~12 years.

From the natal Moon, classically: 1st - discomfort and expenses, but spiritual growth (Jupiter over the Moon is not the "good" transit popular astrology claims); 2nd - wealth and family growth; 3rd - obstacles, effort; 4th - domestic sorrow, relocation; 5th - children, honour; 6th - difficulties, debt; 7th - auspicious: marriage, partnership expansion; 8th - obstacles, inheritance matters; 9th - the most auspicious of all: fortune, dharma, guru, children; 10th - respectable exertions, career expansion; 11th - excellent: gains, income, wishes fulfilled; 12th - expenses, foreign residence, liberation.

In practice, Jupiter's transit is judged mostly for: **marriage** (Jupiter arriving in the 7th house, 7th from the Moon, or aspecting the 7th or Venus), **children**, **education**, and the general "expansion window" of the year. A Jupiter return (every ~12 years, ages ~12, 24, 36, 48) brings a new cycle of growth and opportunity.

## Rahu and Ketu

Nodes move retrograde through the zodiac in ~18.6 years, staying ~1.5 years per sign. **Rahu return** (~18, 37, 55) is a classic "career/desire" marker; **Ketu return** is a marker of detachment and inner reorientation. Rahu transiting the 1st, 5th, 9th or the Lagna itself brings restlessness and sudden developments; Ketu's transit over the natal Moon is an inward, sometimes disorienting period known as Ketu's "transit over the mind".

## Sun, Mars, Mercury, Venus - shorter transits

These are used for **monthly and weekly timing** rather than life phases: the Sun spends ~1 month per sign (~30 days), Mercury ~3 weeks (16-30 days), Venus ~3 weeks to a month, Mars ~45 days. Mars transits are watched for accidents, surgery, disputes and property transactions, especially when Mars crosses the Lagna, the 4th, the 7th, the 8th or the natal Moon (and the 2nd/12th from the Moon). The Sun's monthly transit marks administrative and health cycles and is the basis of the Tamil "sankranti" months.

## How to combine dasha and transit

A classical maxim: **dasha shows the era, transit shows the moment**. The strongest event predictions come from agreement:
- the AD/PD lord is a significator of the matter, AND
- its transit (or Jupiter/Saturn's transit) touches the relevant house, and
- the dasha is in its middle or final phase of that lord.

Give answers as: "The window opens in [antardasha] (dates), and Jupiter's entry into your 7th house in [month/year] is the trigger - expect developments then."

## Useful transit anchors for common questions

- **Marriage**: Jupiter or Saturn touching the 7th house / 7th lord / Venus; Rahu entering the 7th (unconventional unions).
- **Career change**: Saturn crossing the 10th or the 10th lord's nakshatra; the Sun's annual passage over the 10th; Rahu in the 10th (sudden shifts).
- **Property**: Saturn or Mars transiting the 4th; Jupiter aspecting the 4th from the 12th, 4th or 8th.
- **Health caution**: Mars/Saturn/Sun across the Lagna sign or its lord's nakshatra, while the dasha lord is the 6th or 8th lord - then advise rest and medical checks, never diagnosis.
- **Education**: Jupiter and Mercury over the 4th/5th; Rahu in the 4th (distraction) or 9th (foreign study).
- **Spiritual turn**: Ketu or Saturn over the 5th, 9th, 12th; Jupiter in the 12th from the Moon.
