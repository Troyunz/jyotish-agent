# Jyotisha foundations - grahas, rashis, bhavas, nakshatras

## The nine grahas and their karakas (significations)

**Surya (Sun)** - atma (soul), father, authority, vitality, bones, heart, government, fame, ego, self-confidence. The Sun is the natural karaka of the 1st, 9th and 10th houses. A strong Sun gives dignity and leadership; an afflicted Sun gives ego conflicts, trouble with father or authority.

**Chandra (Moon)** - manas (mind), mother, emotions, memory, liquids, blood, the public, nourishment, comfort, receptivity. Karaka of the 4th house. A strong Moon gives emotional stability and popularity; a weak or afflicted Moon gives anxiety, mood swings and dependency. The Moon is the most important graha for judging the mind - more than the Lagna for psychological questions.

**Mangala (Mars)** - parakrama (courage), siblings (especially younger brothers), land and property, energy, surgery, weapons, competition, sports, engineering, anger, accidents, blood. Karaka of the 3rd and 6th houses. Strong Mars gives drive and property; afflicted Mars gives disputes, injuries and hot temper.

**Budha (Mercury)** - buddhi (intellect), speech, commerce, writing, mathematics, accountancy, education, skin, nerves. Karaka of the 4th? No - Mercury is the karaka of speech and intellect, associated with the 3rd, 6th and 10th by nature. Strong Mercury gives eloquence and business skill; afflicted Mercury gives nervousness, deceit or speech problems.

**Guru (Jupiter/Brihaspati)** - jnana (wisdom), children (especially sons), guru, dharma, wealth, expansion, liver, fat, fortune, higher education, law, spirituality. Karaka of the 2nd, 5th and 9th houses. Strong Jupiter protects almost everything it touches; afflicted Jupiter gives arrogance, over-indulgence and difficulties with children or teachers.

**Shukra (Venus)** - kama (desire), spouse, romance, beauty, art, music, vehicles, luxury, semen/ovaries, comfort, diplomacy. Karaka of the 7th house. Strong Venus gives beauty, harmony and marital happiness; afflicted Venus gives relationship troubles and excess indulgence.

**Shani (Saturn)** - karma, discipline, patience, longevity, labour, servants, sorrow, delay, detachment, old age, bones, nerves, chronic conditions. Karaka of the 8th and 12th houses and of longevity and suffering. Saturn never destroys - it delays, tests, then rewards maturity. Strong Saturn gives endurance and lasting achievement; afflicted Saturn gives chronic sorrow, delays and fear, but also often late but durable success.

**Rahu** - obsession, foreign things, technology, illusion, ambition, sudden rise, poison, unorthodox paths, paternal grandfather. Rahu behaves like its dispositor and like the planet it sits with or aspects. It gives material success with dissatisfaction - "more, but never enough".

**Ketu** - detachment, moksha, occult, past-life skills, sudden loss, mysticism, maternal grandfather, healing. Ketu gives sharp intuition but indifference to the very thing its house signifies. Ketu's placement shows where you are naturally gifted but never fully satisfied.

## Benefics and malefics

- Natural benefics: Jupiter, Venus, unafflicted Mercury, waxing Moon.
- Natural malefics: Saturn, Mars, Sun, Rahu, Ketu, waning Moon, afflicted Mercury.
- Mercury is benefic when with benefics, malefic when with malefics.
- Functional benefics and malefics depend on the Lagna: for a Taurus Lagna, Saturn rules the 9th and 10th and becomes a great functional benefic; for Aries Lagna, Saturn rules 10th and 11th and is generally difficult. Always judge by both natural and functional nature, then blend.

## Rashis (signs), elements and qualities

Chara (movable): Aries, Cancer, Libra, Capricorn. Sthira (fixed): Taurus, Leo, Scorpio, Aquarius. Dvisvabhava (dual): Gemini, Virgo, Sagittarius, Pisces.

Agni (fire): Aries, Leo, Sagittarius. Prithvi (earth): Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn. Vayu (air): Gemini, Libra, Aquarius. Jala (water): Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces.

Odd signs (1,3,5,7,9,11) are male/krura (harsh); even signs are female/saumya (gentle). Signs rise with head (Aries, Leo, Sagittarius... sirshodaya), both (Gemini, Libra, Aquarius), feet (Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn... prishthodaya), etc. - relevant for Lagna strength.

Signs and their lords: Aries-Mars, Taurus-Venus, Gemini-Mercury, Cancer-Moon, Leo-Sun, Virgo-Mercury, Libra-Venus, Scorpio-Mars, Sagittarius-Jupiter, Capricorn-Saturn, Aquarius-Saturn, Pisces-Jupiter.

## Bhavas (houses) and their significations

1. **Tanu bhava** - body, self, personality, vitality, head, general fortune. The Lagna is the "head of the kalapurusha".
2. **Dhana bhava** - wealth stored, family, speech, right eye, food habits, values.
3. **Sahaja bhava** - younger siblings, courage, arms, short journeys, communication, skills, self-effort.
4. **Sukha bhava** - mother, home, property, vehicles, education (early), emotional base, chest.
5. **Putra bhava** - children, intelligence, purva punya (past merit), romance, creativity, authority (as 10th from 8th).
6. **Ari bhava** - enemies, disease, debts, service, competition, litigation, daily work, digestion.
7. **Kalatra bhava** - spouse, partnerships, business dealings, public relations, sexual life, travel (as 10th from 10th).
8. **Randhra bhava** - longevity, death, inheritance, occult, transformation, chronic illness, research, in-laws, hidden wealth.
9. **Dharma bhava** - fortune (bhagya), father, guru, higher learning, long journeys, pilgrimage, dharma, hips.
10. **Karma bhava** - career, status, karma in the world, authority, reputation, father (as alternate), knees.
11. **Labha bhava** - gains, income, elder siblings, friends, fulfilment of desires, ankles.
12. **Vyaya bhava** - loss, expenditure, foreign residence, moksha, imagination, sleep, bed pleasures, isolation, feet.

House classification: kendras (angles) 1,4,7,10 - the pillars of the chart (Vishnu sthanas). Trikonas (trines) 1,5,9 - Lakshmi sthanas, the most auspicious. Upachaya (growing) houses 3,6,10,11 - malefics here improve with time. Dusthanas 6,8,12 - houses of difficulty; also 3 and 11 are "mildly malefic" because they are the 8th/12th from... (as 3rd from 1st is 8th from... ) - traditionally 3, 6, 8, 11, 12 are the "evil" houses in some enumerations.

Duplicate houses: the 3rd is the 8th from the 8th; the 6th is the 3rd from the 3rd; the 8th is the 12th from the 9th, etc. A house also represents other houses' matters (e.g., 10th = 4th from the 7th = the spouse's home).

Marakas: lords of the 2nd and 7th can act as marakas (killers) in longevity analysis - a technical matter to be handled with care and never used to predict death.

## Karaka planets for specific questions

Marriage: Jupiter for a woman, Venus for a man; both plus the 7th lord and Darakaraka (Jaimini). Children: Jupiter (putrakaraka), 5th house and 5th lord. Career: 10th lord, Sun, Saturn (karma), Mercury (business), Amatyakaraka. Wealth: Jupiter (Dhana karaka), 2nd and 11th lords, Lakshmi yoga. Education: Mercury and Jupiter, 4th and 5th houses. Mother: Moon and 4th house. Father: Sun and 9th house. Enemies: Mars, 6th house. Moksha: Ketu, Jupiter, 12th house. Longevity: Saturn is the karaka of longevity, with the 8th house and the Ayurdaya calculations (avoid direct claims about lifespan; instead discuss health and vitality).

## Graha drishti (planetary aspects)

All grahas fully aspect the 7th house from themselves. Special aspects: **Mars** also aspects the 4th and 8th; **Jupiter** the 5th and 9th; **Saturn** the 3rd and 10th. Rahu and Ketu are given 5th, 7th and 9th aspects by many modern authorities (schools differ - the classical texts give the nodes no special aspect). An aspect is strongest on the nearest point and weakest at maximum orb; in Jyotisha aspects are counted in whole signs, not by degrees.

## Planetary states - dignity and condition

- **Uccha (exaltation)**: Sun 10 Aries, Moon 3 Taurus, Mars 28 Capricorn, Mercury 15 Virgo, Jupiter 5 Cancer, Venus 27 Pisces, Saturn 20 Libra. The opposite point is neecha (debilitation).
- **Moolatrikona** (a sign where the planet is very comfortable and strong): Sun Leo 0-20, Moon Taurus 4-30, Mars Aries 0-12, Mercury Virgo 16-20, Jupiter Sagittarius 0-10, Venus Libra 0-15, Saturn Aquarius 0-20.
- **Swakshetra** (own sign) - see sign lords above.
- **Mitra/shatru** (friend/enemy signs) - judged by natural friendship (e.g. Sun's friends: Moon, Mars, Jupiter; enemies: Venus, Saturn) tempered by temporary friendship from sign positions in the chart (tatkalika).
- **Combustion** (asta): a planet too close to the Sun loses lustre. Common orbs: Moon 12, Mars 17, Mercury 14, Jupiter 11, Venus 10, Saturn 15 degrees.
- **Vakri (retrograde)**: retrograde planets are strong in a "cheshta" (motional) sense - they act with unusual intensity, often inward or against the current. Retrograde Jupiter or Saturn frequently shows revisiting of a life theme.
- **Vargottama**: same sign in Rashi and Navamsa - a mark of consistency.
- **Digbala**: Jupiter and Mercury are strong in the east (1st house), Sun and Mars in the south (10th), Saturn in the west (7th), Moon and Venus in the north (4th).

## Nakshatras (27 lunar mansions) and their lords

1. Ashwini - Ketu; 2. Bharani - Venus; 3. Krittika - Sun; 4. Rohini - Moon; 5. Mrigashira - Mars; 6. Ardra - Rahu; 7. Punarvasu - Jupiter; 8. Pushya - Saturn; 9. Ashlesha - Mercury; 10. Magha - Ketu; 11. Purva Phalguni - Venus; 12. Uttara Phalguni - Sun; 13. Hasta - Moon; 14. Chitra - Mars; 15. Swati - Rahu; 16. Vishakha - Jupiter; 17. Anuradha - Saturn; 18. Jyeshtha - Mercury; 19. Mula - Ketu; 20. Purva Ashadha - Venus; 21. Uttara Ashadha - Sun; 22. Shravana - Moon; 23. Dhanishta - Mars; 24. Shatabhisha - Rahu; 25. Purva Bhadrapada - Jupiter; 26. Uttara Bhadrapada - Saturn; 27. Revati - Mercury.

Each nakshatra spans 13°20' and is divided into four padas (quarters) of 3°20'. The navamsa sign is derived from the pada: Ashwini pada 1 = Aries navamsa, and so on. Nakshatra of the Moon (janma nakshatra) determines the Vimshottari dasha sequence, and the pada refines the personality considerably. Nakshatras are classified by gana (deva/manushya/rakshasa), yoni, nadi and varna - all used in marriage matching.

## Rashi and nakshatra in practice

- **Janma rashi** (Moon sign) is what traditional Indian astrology uses for daily transits and for matching.
- **Lagna rashi** is used for the body, personality and house-based analysis.
- **Surya rashi** (Sun sign) is general and collective.
- The Moon changes sign every ~2.25 days and nakshatra every ~24 hours - so an error of a few hours in birth time usually does not change the Moon sign but can change the nakshatra pada and, near a boundary, the nakshatra itself.

## Vargas (divisional charts) - which to use for what

- **D1 Rashi** - the base chart, the body and everything at the material level.
- **D2 Hora** - wealth and resources.
- **D3 Drekkana** - siblings, courage, co-borns.
- **D4 Chaturthamsa** - property, home, fortune.
- **D7 Saptamsa** - children and progeny.
- **D9 Navamsa** - the "second half of life", marriage, dharma, inner strength, the true worth of a planet. A planet strong in D1 but weak in D9 promises less than it appears.
- **D10 Dashamsa** - career, karma, status, actions.
- **D12 Dwadashamsa** - parents, lineage.
- **D16 Shodashamsa** - vehicles, comforts, luxuries.
- **D20 Vimsamsa** - spiritual practice, upasana.
- **D24 Siddhamsa/Chaturvimsamsa** - education and learning.
- **D27 Bhamsa** - strengths and weaknesses, vitality.
- **D30 Trimsamsa** - evils, misfortunes, moral nature.
- **D40 Khavedamsa** - auspicious/inauspicious maternal legacy.
- **D45 Akshavedamsa** - paternal legacy, general character.
- **D60 Shashtiamsa** - the finest karma; extremely sensitive to birth time accuracy.

Rule of thumb: D1 shows the potential, the relevant varga shows the actual outcome for that life area. Always judge the varga along with the D1 position of the same planet; a planet weak in D1 but exalted in D9 can deliver results later in life.

## Ashtakoota (marriage matching, 36 points)

Varna (1), Vashya (2), Tara/Dina (3), Yoni (4), Graha Maitri (5), Gana (6), Bhakoot (7), Nadi (8) - total 36 points. Above 18 is generally considered acceptable, 24+ good, 28+ excellent. Bhakoot and Nadi are weighted most heavily because they affect health, progeny and longevity of the marriage. However, classical texts allow exceptions: if Rashi lords are friends, if the 7th lords of both charts are strong, or if both have the same Rashi lord, some doshas (especially Bhakoot) are cancelled. Matching is a guide, never a veto over two people's free choice.

## Panchanga basics

Tithi (lunar day, 12° Moon-Sun elongation each; 30 per lunation), Vara (weekday), Nakshatra (Moon's mansion), Nitya Yoga (Sun+Moon longitude, 27), Karana (half-tithi, 60 per lunation). Among tithis, Amavasya, Ashtami and Chaturdashi are considered difficult (rikta), and Purnima is auspicious. The panchanga is judged for muhurta (electional astrology) - marriage muhurtas avoid Bhadra (Vishti karana), rikta tithis and certain nakshatras such as Bharani, Krittika, Magha, and prefer Uttarayana, Jupiter/Venus strong, and the 7th house unafflicted.

## How to read a chart - the classical order

1. Lagna, its lord's placement and strength - the "native's own condition".
2. The Moon (mind) and the Sun (soul/authority) and any affliction to them.
3. For the topic asked: the relevant bhava, its lord, planets in it, planets aspecting it, and the karaka planet.
4. Yogas (combinations) formed - see the yogas file.
5. Dasha (timing) - the running periods and their lords' relation to the bhava asked about.
6. Gochara (transits) from the Moon.
7. Relevant varga for refinement.
8. Synthesise; never decide from a single factor. A statement built on two or three concordant factors is strong; one factor alone is a hint.
