# Classical yogas and doshas - definitions and how to judge them

A yoga is a specific configuration of grahas that produces a definite result. A dosha is a configuration traditionally considered difficult, usually with classical exceptions (bhanga) that cancel or soften it. Always state the definition, then check the cancellations before saying anything alarming.

## Pancha Mahapurusha yogas

Formed when Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus or Saturn is in **own sign, moolatrikona or exaltation, and in a kendra (1, 4, 7, 10) from the Lagna**:

- **Ruchaka** (Mars) - commanding presence, physical courage, victory in competition, leadership in action.
- **Bhadra** (Mercury) - intelligence, eloquence, learning, business acumen, longevity.
- **Hamsa** (Jupiter) - wisdom, respected speech, spiritual inclination, good family and fortune.
- **Malavya** (Venus) - beauty, comfort, vehicles, artistic gifts, marital happiness.
- **Sasa** (Saturn) - authority over people, endurance, gains through labour, leadership of institutions.

If the planet is exalted the yoga is strongest; in own sign strong; in moolatrikona strong. If the planet is also combust or defeated in planetary war, the yoga is weak or cancelled.

## Gajakesari yoga

Jupiter in a kendra (1, 4, 7, 10) from the Moon. Gives wisdom, reputation, protection in crisis, eloquence and support from mentors. Stronger when Jupiter is also dignified and the Moon is well disposed.

## Budha-Aditya yoga

Sun and Mercury in the same sign (Mercury not too close to be burnt). Sharp intellect, analytical ability, good communication, success in administrative, academic or business work. Cancelled/weakened if Mercury is combust beyond orb or in a dusthana with a malefic.

## Chandra-Mangala yoga

Moon conjunct Mars. Gives enterprise, wealth earned through one's own effort, decisiveness. Also gives restlessness, sharp speech, a tendency to overreact and (in the classical texts) an interest in other people's partners - to be mentioned only as "impulsiveness in relationships", not as a moral verdict.

## Moon-support yogas: Sunapha, Anapha, Durudhura, Kemadruma

Counted from the Moon, excluding the Sun:

- **Sunapha** - planet(s) in the 2nd from the Moon: self-earned wealth, well-formed character.
- **Anapha** - planet(s) in the 12th from the Moon: renunciant streak, self-restraint, fame.
- **Durudhura** - planets on both sides of the Moon: comfort, generosity, balanced support.
- **Kemadruma** - no planet in the 2nd or 12th from the Moon, none with the Moon: mental isolation, struggles and a self-reliant mind. **Cancellations**: a planet in a kendra from the Lagna or from the Moon; the Moon herself in a kendra; benefics aspecting the Moon; the Moon conjunct or aspected by Jupiter - then the yoga is void, and the native is simply introspective.

## Adhi yoga and Amala yoga

- **Adhi yoga** - benefics (Jupiter, Venus, Mercury, or a strong Moon) in the 6th, 7th and 8th from the Moon: leadership, health, wealth and defeat of enemies. With two benefics it gives a chief's position; with all three, a minister-like rise.
- **Amala yoga** - a natural benefic in the 10th from the Moon or from the Lagna: clean reputation, lasting good name, charitable work recognised publicly.

## Raja and Dhana yogas

- **Raja yoga (kendra-trikona sambandha)** - any connection (conjunction, mutual aspect, or sign exchange) between a lord of houses 1/4/7/10 and a lord of houses 1/5/9. The more such connections and the stronger the planets, the higher the position promised. Timing: the dasha of the involved planets.
- **Dhana yogas** - connections among lords of 2, 5, 9, 11; the 2nd lord strong with the 11th lord; the 5th and 9th lords conjunct; a strong 9th lord (Lakshmi yoga). Wealth arrives during the relevant dashas, and is preserved only if the 2nd house is strong.
- **Parivartana (exchange) yoga** - two planets swap signs. **Maha parivartana** between good houses (1,2,4,5,7,9,10,11) is a strong positive; **Dainya** and **Khala** parivartana involving 6/8/12 bring ups and downs; the two houses are always linked in experience.

## Vipareeta Raja yogas

Because the lords of 6, 8 and 12 are "difficult", their own occupation of these houses turns adversity into advantage:

- **Harsha** - 6th lord in 6, 8 or 12: victory over enemies and disease, gains through litigation or service.
- **Sarala** - 8th lord in 6, 8 or 12: longevity, freedom from fear, gains through inheritance or insurance, success in occult research.
- **Vimala** - 12th lord in 6, 8 or 12: independence, frugality that becomes wealth, benefits from foreign lands.

## Nabhasa and pattern yogas (brief)

Concentration of planets in movable signs (Rajju - travel and restlessness), fixed signs (Musala - steadiness and wealth), dual signs (Mala - comfort), all in houses 1-4 (Gada), 4-7 (Sakata - an up-and-down life), 7-10 (Vihanga), 10-1 (Sringataka), all in kendras (Vajra or Yava depending on benefic/malefic), all in trikonas (Kamala - lotus: prosperity and virtue), all in a row across contiguous houses (Chatra, Chapa...). Use these only as flavour, since they describe the overall shape of the chart rather than a specific event.

## Difficult combinations worth naming carefully

- **Shakata yoga** - Moon and Jupiter in mutual 6/8: fortunes ebb and flow; wealth needs conscious management; the classical remedy is Jupiter's grace through dharma.
- **Kala Sarpa / Kala Amrita** - all seven grahas hemmed between Rahu and Ketu. **Not a classical yoga** (it is absent from BPHS, Phaladeepika, Saravali and Brihat Jataka); it entered the popular tradition recently. Present it as a symbolic emphasising of Rahu-Ketu's axis in life, and state clearly that the classical texts do not treat it as a curse. When the person is doing astrological counselling, honesty here is essential.
- **Grahan yoga** - a luminary conjunct Rahu or Ketu. Eclipses the significations of the Sun (father, self-confidence, authority) or Moon (mother, mind, emotional security). Classical remedies are spiritual practice, worship of the deity of the nakshatra, and charity - never fear.
- **Daridra yoga** - the 11th lord in a dusthana with a malefic: income leaks; poverty tendencies unless counteracted by the 2nd/9th strength.
- **Kemadruma, already covered** - always check the cancellations first.

## Mangal dosha (Kuja dosha) - with the classical exceptions

Mars in houses **1, 2, 4, 7, 8 or 12** from the Lagna (or from the Moon, or from Venus by some schools; many authorities count from all three and weigh accordingly) is said to affect marriage: friction, dominance struggles, delay, or a partner's health. Classical exceptions (cancellations) - state these before the difficulty:

1. Mars in its own sign (Aries, Scorpio) or exaltation (Capricorn).
2. Mars in Cancer (the 4th house from a Cancer Lagna softens it considerably).
3. Mars conjunct or aspected by Jupiter, or Jupiter in a kendra from the Lagna.
4. Mars in the 2nd/7th when the 7th house or its lord is strong and well supported.
5. Both partners have the same dosha (a traditional cancellation - the matching cancels out).
6. Mars is in a movable sign at the Lagna while the 7th is fixed, or similar, per some texts.

Modern practice: with any of the above, do not call it a defect in the person; describe the relationship dynamics it indicates (assertiveness, impatience, need for space) and propose practical patience rather than fear. Never let it veto a match on its own.

## Neecha bhanga (cancellation of debilitation)

A debilitated planet loses its harm when:
- The lord of the debilitation sign is in a kendra from the Lagna or the Moon, or is exalted.
- The debilitated planet is aspected by or conjunct its dispositor or its exaltation lord.
- The debilitated planet is in a kendra and lagna lord is strong.
- The planet that would be exalted in that sign (the "neecha bhanga planet") is strong in a kendra.

Such a planet often behaves like an exalted one - the classically quoted paradox is that a neecha-bhanga planet can give more than a merely strong one, because the person has struggled with that area and mastered it.

## Vargottama and pushkara matters

**Vargottama** (same position in D1 and D9) strengthens a planet significantly - the matter it rules is steady and reliable. **Pushkara navamsa/bhaga** (specific auspicious navamsa positions) further refine strength; mention only if computed.
